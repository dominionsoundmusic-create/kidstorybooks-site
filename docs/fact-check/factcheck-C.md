# Fact-check C: story-books-by-age, coloring-books-for-kids, books-for-teens, about

Checked 2026-11-12 by an independent sub-agent that did not write the pages. Method: research claims were checked with WebSearch limited to each cited organization's own domain. **Every SUPPORTED verdict is based on search results (titles and snippets) from that domain. No page was opened directly, because the sandbox blocks WebFetch.** Book claims were checked against data/books.json. Business facts were checked against INTAKE.md. KJV quotes were checked from memory against the KJV text. The project's tools/check_kjv.py is the authoritative KJV check.

## Must fix

1. **coloring #11**: "Animal Crossword Puzzle and Fruit Word Puzzle mix words and pictures."
   Change to: "Animal Crossword Puzzle and Fruit Word Puzzle are word puzzle books, as their titles say."
2. **coloring #15** (FAQ "What coloring books work for 9 and 10 year olds?"): this implies an age fit for specific books.
   Change the answer to: "Older kids often want more detail and a theme they care about. The site does not list an age for its coloring books, so check each book's Amazon page. Word puzzle books like Animal Crossword Puzzle and Fruit Word Puzzle are one option for kids who like words."
   You could also reword the question so it does not ask for an age match.
3. **teens #12**: "A slim book of daily affirmations" makes a page-count claim.
   Change to: "A book of daily affirmations or a book that takes on one big question can feel less like homework than a long novel."
4. **teens #13** (FAQ "Are there Christian books for teens?"): only the Quiet Strength titles signal faith.
   Change to: "Yes. The Quiet Strength books (Volumes 1 to 5) have faith themes in their titles, such as God Made My Mind. The teen shelf also includes Daily Affirmations for Victorious Teens, The Secret To Unshakable Truth! and Navigating Life's Deep Questions: Why Do Bad Things Happen?"
5. **story-by-age #3**: the AAP "from birth" point.
   Change to: "The AAP suggests sturdy board books, soft cloth books and vinyl or bath books for babies. Chewing on books is normal..." (drop "from birth").
