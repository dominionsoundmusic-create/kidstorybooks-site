Children's books to read together

These children's books are made for reading out loud: on the couch after school, at the kitchen table, at church, or in bed before the lights go out.

Maurice Johnson writes for parents, grandparents, teachers and churches who want good stories for the children in their lives. Many of his books are Christian picture books. Others are about animals, feelings, family, the natural world and the holidays. There are also cookbooks, coloring and puzzle books, and a shelf of books for teens.

The site does not sell anything itself. Each book has its own page on Amazon, where you can see the formats, the current price and the shipping options. Pick a topic below to find books that fit your child, or search the full list.
