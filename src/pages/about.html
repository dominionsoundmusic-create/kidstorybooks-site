Maurice Johnson, children's author and publisher

Maurice Johnson is the children's author behind every title on this site. He lives in East Texas and publishes his books through Dominion Publishing, part of Dominion Digital Group.

Maurice is a Christian, and faith runs through much of his work. Sixty-five of his books are Christian titles, including Bible stories, books on the names of God, and stories about prayer, grace and thankful hearts.

He also writes far beyond Sunday school. His catalogue includes children's stories about animals, family and feelings; nature and life cycle books; coloring and puzzle books; cookbooks; and books for teens.

Why a site that sends you to Amazon

Kid Story Books is a front door to Maurice's books, not a store. Amazon handles the printing, payment and delivery, and each book's Amazon page shows the formats and price that are available right now.

That keeps things simple for parents, grandparents, teachers and churches: find a book here, then buy it on Amazon the way you already shop.
